# Demo narration (ElevenLabs)

Segmented to the timeline in `demo-video-plan.md`. About 330 words. Numbers and the URL are spelled out for the voice model.

```
[0:00 Intro, over the title]
Dukkan. A shop that AI agents buy from.
Dukkan lets someone who sells crafts from home, on her phone or her desktop, in her own language, open a shop that AI shopping agents can search and buy from. PayPal moves the money.

[0:10 Salma's workshop]
This is Salma. She makes cushions. She will never build a website. This is her whole shop. She only checks, confirms, and ships.

[0:16 Onboarding and Connect PayPal]
She names her shop, picks her language, and connects her own PayPal account with Log in with PayPal. Dukkan never sees a password. Every payment goes straight to her.

[0:40 Add a product]
To add a product, she takes a photo and records a voice note. Gemini transcribes it. Claude writes the English listing. Anything the AI guessed is kept apart, so she can check it. She answers one question about stock, and publishes.

[1:45 The flight to the buyer]
Her buyers are not people browsing. They are AI agents.

[1:55 The agent buys]
This agent talks to Dukkan only through the public API, the same way Perplexity or Copilot would. It searches the catalogue, builds a cart, and asks PayPal to create the order. The agent never pays by itself. It hands the human a pay link, and she approves on PayPal's own button.

[2:20 Money lands]
Back on Salma's screen, the payment is captured and the order appears. No refresh. No dashboard. The coin drops.

[2:30 Ship and refund]
She opens the order and adds a tracking number. Dukkan pushes it to PayPal's order tracking. If something goes wrong before shipping, one tap refunds the buyer through the Payments API, and puts the item back in stock.

[2:50 Outro]
Any agent can do what you just saw. The API is public, documented in OpenAPI, and live at dukkan dash eight dot vercel dot app. Dukkan. A seller speaks, AI writes the listing, agents buy, PayPal pays.
```

ElevenLabs settings: narrator voice, high stability, low style. If "PayPal" or "Dukkan" is mispronounced, write "Pay Pal" / "Dook-kan" in the text.
